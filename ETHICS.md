# 🔒 Ethics and Social Considerations

## Overview

MathQuest raises several important ethical and social considerations 
due to its target audience of children aged 3 to 15. As Team Leader 
I researched and documented these considerations throughout the project.

---

## Data Privacy

### GDPR Compliance
- No personal data collected at prototype stage
- Future: parental consent required before account creation
- Progress data stored locally only
- No third-party data sharing

### COPPA Compliance
- No data collection from under-13 users without parental consent
- No behavioural advertising
- Privacy policy must be parent-accessible

---

## Social Impact

### Positive Impact
- Improves mathematical literacy in children
- Accessible across multiple age groups
- Engaging format reduces maths anxiety
- Can support classroom and home learning equally

### Potential Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Screen time addiction | Session time limits planned for future version |
| Difficulty frustrating young learners | Hint system and gradual difficulty increase |
| Exclusion of children with learning difficulties | Accessibility features planned for future |
| Data privacy breach | Local-only storage, no accounts at prototype stage |
| Socioeconomic access gap | Flutter enables free app distribution on both platforms |

---

## Accessibility Considerations

Future versions of MathQuest should include:
- Audio narration for pre-readers aged 3-5
- High contrast mode for visual impairments
- Dyslexia-friendly font options
- Larger text and button options
- Keyboard/switch navigation support
